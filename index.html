GOOD TO KNOW

Questions clients ask

How fast is a typical turnaround?

Short-form reels usually land in 24–48 hours; longer edits take 4–7 days depending on length and how much footage there is. If you have a hard deadline, say so in your brief — I’ll tell you honestly whether I can hit it before you commit to anything.

What do you need from me to start?

A Drive or Dropbox link with your footage, any brand assets (logo, fonts, colours) and one or two references you like. That is usually enough for a first cut.

How many revisions do I get?

Two rounds on every edit, and I turn notes around fast. Send them in one message if you can.

Do you work with clients in other time zones?

Yes. Almost everything runs async over WhatsApp or email, and I will jump on a call whenever that is faster than typing.

Who owns the final files?

You do. You get the finished exports in every format your channels need, and the source project is available on request. Happy to sign an NDA.

Not covered here? WhatsApp me — usually the fastest way to get a straight answer.
